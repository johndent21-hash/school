In this chapter you will

  • interpret picture, column, sector and line graphs
  • identify misleading graphs
  • read and draw dot plots and stem-and-leaf plots
  • find the mean, mode, median and range
  • analyse data shown in plots
  • compare two sets of data

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is snowflake-like branches between the main body and the top bulb. Maths can make beautiful things.
