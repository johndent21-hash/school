2Then work down the columns, left to right. They get harder as you go: Easy Easy Medium on page 1, then Medium Challenging Challenging on page 2.
3Most questions practise today's skill. A question marked from 1.03 comes from an earlier lesson, to keep it fresh. Stuck? Look back at that lesson.

In this chapter you will

  • use grid references on a map
  • plot and read points in the first quadrant
  • plot and read points in all four quadrants
  • plot points from tables and describe patterns
  • complete tables of values from rules
  • graph linear equations and test whether a point is on a line

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is the whole set, drawn on the complex number plane. Maths can make beautiful things.
