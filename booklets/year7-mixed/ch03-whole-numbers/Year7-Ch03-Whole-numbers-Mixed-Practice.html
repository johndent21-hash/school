How to use this booklet

2Then work down the columns, left to right. They get harder as you go: Easy Easy Medium on page 1, then Medium Challenging Challenging on page 2.

  • round and estimate

  • use divisibility tests
  • use long division and Roman numerals
  • use index notation, square roots and cube roots
  • find prime numbers and prime factors
  • find the HCF and LCM of two numbers

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is the tentacled edge of a small copy of the whole set, out on the antenna near −1.77. Maths can make beautiful things.
