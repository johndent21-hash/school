How to use this booklet

1Each lesson starts with learning intentions and success criteria. Write the key terms as your teacher explains them, and use the notes box.
2Work through the levels in order: Level 1 , then Level 2, then Level 3. Extension questions stretch you further.
WE DOGrey boxes with a dark border. Work with your teacher. Copy what they write on the board.
YOU DOBlue boxes. Work on your own. Show your working in the box and write your answer.
3Some questions ask you to draw a diagram, graph or construction. Use the space given, a ruler and a sharp pencil.
4At the end of each lesson, show off your skill: choose one question, then tear off the ticket and hand it in.
5Missed a lesson? Each lesson ends with a summary that has a worked example for every level. Start there, then try the questions.

In this chapter you will

  • measure and draw angles with a protractor
  • classify angles by size
  • find complementary, supplementary and vertically opposite angles
  • construct parallel and perpendicular lines
  • find corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles on parallel lines
  • prove that two lines are parallel

Worked example
The hands of a clock show 4 o’clock. What is the angle between them? What is the reflex angle?
  1. Each hour mark is 360 ÷ 12 = 30°
  2. 4 hours apart: 4 × 30 = 120°
  3. Reflex: 360 − 120 = 240°

Answer: 120° and reflex 240°

YOU DOOn your own: read the worked example, then try these.
E1Find the angle between the hands at 5 o’clock and at 9 o’clock.
E2At 3:30 the hour hand is halfway between 3 and 4. Find the angle between the hands.
Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Remember
  1. Centre of the protractor on the vertex.
  2. 0° line along one arm.
  3. Count up from 0° on that scale.
  4. Reflex: measure the small angle, then 360° − it.
Level 1
Measure a 64° angle
  1. Centre on vertex, 0° on arm
  2. Read the scale that starts at 0°

Answer: 64°

Level 2
Draw 117°
  1. Draw an arm, mark 117°, join to the vertex

Answer: an obtuse angle

Level 3
Draw 210°
  1. 360 − 210 = 150
  2. Draw 150° the other way

Answer: a reflex angle

Extension
Angle at 4 o’clock
  1. 4 × 30°

Answer: 120°

Extension · Stretch yourself
Worked example
Find the angle whose supplement is 3 times its complement.
  1. Let the angle be x
  2. 180 − x = 3(90 − x)
  3. 180 − x = 270 − 3x, so 2x = 90

Answer: x = 45°

YOU DOOn your own: read the worked example, then try these.

  2. Supplementary angles add to 180°.
  3. Right angle split in two → complementary.
  4. Straight line split in two → supplementary.
