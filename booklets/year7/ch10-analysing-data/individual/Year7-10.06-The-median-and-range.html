Worked example
25 values are written in order. Which value is the median?
  1. Add 1 to the number of values: 25 + 1 = 26
  2. Halve it: 26 ÷ 2 = 13
  3. The median is the 13th value (12 values sit on each side)

Key idea: the median is in position (n + 1) ÷ 2. If that ends in .5, average the two values either side.

Answer: the 13th value

Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Remember
  1. Put the data in order first.
  2. Median = the middle value.
  3. Two middle values? Add them and divide by 2.
  4. Range = highest − lowest.
Level 1
Find the median of 7, 3, 9, 5, 4
  1. Order: 3, 4, 5, 7, 9
  2. Middle (3rd) value: 5

Answer: 5

Level 2
Find the median and range of 6, 2, 9, 4
  1. Order: 2, 4, 6, 9
  2. Two middle values: (4 + 6) ÷ 2 = 5
  3. Range: 9 − 2 = 7

Answer: median 5, range 7

Level 3
Temperatures ranged from −3 °C to 8 °C. Find the range.
  1. Range = highest − lowest
  2. 8 − (−3) = 8 + 3 = 11

Answer: 11 °C

Extension
Where is the median of 31 ordered values?
  1. (31 + 1) ÷ 2 = 16

Answer: the 16th value
