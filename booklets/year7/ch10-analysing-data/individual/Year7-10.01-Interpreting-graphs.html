Worked example
In a survey of 72 students, the Soccer sector of a sector graph has an angle of 90°. How many students chose soccer?
  1. A full circle is 360°, so 90° is 90/360 = ¼ of the circle
  2. ¼ of 72 = 72 ÷ 4 = 18

Answer: 18 students chose soccer

Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Reading any graph
  1. Read the title: what is the graph about?
  2. Check the key or the scale.
  3. Use a ruler to line up bars and points with the scale.
  4. Answer with the right units (students, °C, %).
Level 1
In a picture graph, = 10 students. A row shows 3½ symbols. How many students is that?
  1. 3 full symbols = 3 × 10 = 30
  2. Half a symbol = 10 ÷ 2 = 5
  3. 30 + 5 = 35

Answer: 35 students

Level 2
A sector graph shows 80 people. The Surfing sector is a quarter of the circle. How many chose surfing?
  1. A quarter means divide by 4
  2. 80 ÷ 4 = 20

Answer: 20 people

Level 3
A divided bar graph shows 45% of 400 students catch the bus. How many students is that?
  1. 45% means 45 out of every 100
  2. 400 has 4 hundreds, so 45 × 4 = 180

Answer: 180 students

Extension
A 120° sector shows the results for 90 people. How many people is that?
  1. 120/360 = ⅓ of the circle
  2. ⅓ of 90 = 90 ÷ 3 = 30

Answer: 30 people
