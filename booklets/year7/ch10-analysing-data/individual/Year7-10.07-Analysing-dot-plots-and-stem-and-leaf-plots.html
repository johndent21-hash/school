Worked example
A dot plot shows 10 values with a mean of 3. Nine of the values are 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5. What is the tenth value?
  1. Total of all 10 values: 10 × 3 = 30
  2. Total of the nine values: 29
  3. Tenth value: 30 − 29 = 1

Answer: 1

Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
From a plot
  1. Range: highest − lowest.
  2. Mode: tallest stack or most repeated leaf.
  3. Median: count to the middle position.
  4. Mean: add every value (value × frequency), then divide.
Level 1
Find the range and mode of the siblings dot plot.
  1. Range: highest − lowest = 5 − 0 = 5
  2. The tallest stack is above 1

Answer: range 5, mode 1

Level 2
Find the median of this plot.
  1. There are 7 values, so the median is the 4th
  2. Count along: 21, 24, 24, 30
StemLeaf
21  4  4
30  5
42  6

Key: 2 | 1 = 21

Answer: 30

Level 3
Find the mean of the siblings dot plot.
  1. Total: 0×3 + 1×5 + 2×4 + 3×2 + 5×1 = 24
  2. There are 15 values: 24 ÷ 15 = 1.6

Answer: 1.6

Extension
Why does an outlier change the mean more than the median?
  1. The mean adds up every value, so one big value adds a lot
  2. The median only uses the middle position

Answer: the outlier pulls the mean towards it
