How to use this booklet

1Each lesson starts with learning intentions and success criteria. Write the key terms as your teacher explains them, and use the notes box.
2Work through the levels in order: Level 1 , then Level 2, then Level 3. Extension questions stretch you further.
WE DOGrey boxes with a dark border. Work with your teacher. Copy what they write on the board.
YOU DOBlue boxes. Work on your own. Show your working in the box and write your answer.
3Some questions ask you to draw a diagram, graph or construction. Use the space given, a ruler and a sharp pencil.
4At the end of each lesson, show off your skill: choose one question, then tear off the ticket and hand it in.
5Missed a lesson? Each lesson ends with a summary that has a worked example for every level. Start there, then try the questions.

Worked example
Light travels 300 000 km in one second. How far does it travel in one hour?

  2. 300 000 × 3600

Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Conversions
  1. km → m × 1000, m → cm × 100, cm → mm × 10.
  2. kg → g × 1000, t → kg × 1000, L → mL × 1000.
  3. Bigger unit → smaller unit: multiply. Smaller → bigger: divide.
  4. Time: 60 s = 1 min, 60 min = 1 h, 24 h = 1 day.
Level 1

  1. ÷ 1000

Answer: 3.2 km

Level 2
8.3 L to mL
  1. × 1000

Answer: 8300 mL

Level 3

  1. 15 × 60 × 60

Answer: 54 000 s

Light in 1 hour

Answer: 1 080 000 000 km

Extension · Stretch yourself
Worked example
A rectangle’s length is 3 times its width. Its perimeter is 64 cm. Find its dimensions.
  1. Width w, length 3w
  2. 2(w + 3w) = 8w = 64
  3. w = 8

Answer: 8 cm by 24 cm

YOU DOOn your own: read the worked example, then try these.
E1A rectangle’s length is 5 cm more than its width. Its perimeter is 50 cm. Find its dimensions.
E2An isosceles triangle has perimeter 40 cm and base 12 cm. Find the equal sides.
Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Remember
  1. Perimeter = distance around the outside.
  2. Add every side length.
  3. Find missing sides using opposite sides.
  4. Check all units are the same.
Level 1
Square, side 13 m
  1. 4 × 13

Answer: 52 m

Level 2
Rectangle 13 mm × 5 mm
  1. 2 × (13 + 5)

Answer: 36 mm

Level 3
L-shape 22, 6, 17…
  1. Missing sides: 22 − 10 = 12, 17 − 6 = 11

Answer: 78 cm

Extension
Length = 3 × width, P = 64
  1. 8w = 64

Answer: 8 cm by 24 cm

  2. C = πd or C = 2πr.

Worked example
Rain of 10 mm falls on a 12 m by 8 m roof. How many litres run into the tank?
  1. 10 mm = 0.01 m
  2. V = 12 × 8 × 0.01 = 0.96 m³
  3. × 1000

Answer: 960 L
