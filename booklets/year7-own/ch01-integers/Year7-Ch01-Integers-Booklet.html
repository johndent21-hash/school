In this chapter you will

  • understand positive and negative numbers and their opposites
  • use a number line to show and compare integers
  • order integers
  • add, subtract, multiply and divide integers
  • use the order of operations with integers, with and without a calculator
  • solve problems involving integers

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is the long antenna of the set along the negative real axis, dotted with tiny copies of the whole set. Maths can make beautiful things.
