In this chapter you will

  • understand positive and negative numbers and their opposites
  • use a number line to show and compare integers
  • order integers
  • add, subtract, multiply and divide integers
  • use the order of operations with integers, with and without a calculator
  • solve problems involving integers

About the circles

Put 10 points around a circle, numbered 0 to 9. Join each point to the point twice its number. After 9, count on from 0 again: so 6 joins to 12, which is point 2. Use 300 points and the lines draw the heart shape on the cover. Lesson 1 uses the 2 times table, lesson 2 the 3 times table, and so on. Count the points of each shape: always one less than the times table.
