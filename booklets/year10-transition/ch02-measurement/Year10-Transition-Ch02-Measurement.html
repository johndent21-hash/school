In this chapter you will

  • convert between units of length, mass, capacity and time
  • find the perimeter of shapes, including composite shapes
  • find the circumference and the area of circles
  • find the area of squares, rectangles, triangles, parallelograms, trapeziums and rhombuses
  • find the surface area of prisms
  • find the volume and capacity of prisms and cylinders

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is feathery gold spirals deep in Elephant Valley, coloured in gold and green. Maths can make beautiful things.
