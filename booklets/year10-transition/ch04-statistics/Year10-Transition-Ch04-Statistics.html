In this chapter you will

  • sort data into categorical and numerical, and name each type
  • organise data in frequency tables with class intervals and percentage frequency
  • read and draw histograms
  • find the mean, median, mode and range
  • read and draw stem-and-leaf plots
  • describe data as symmetrical or skewed
  • read time-series graphs and describe trends

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is a band of gold triple spirals on the edge of the lower bulb, coloured in gold and green. Maths can make beautiful things.

Histograms

Date

Learning intention

We are learning to read a histogram, and draw one from a frequency table.

Success criteria

  • I can read the frequency of a class from a histogram
  • I can find the most common class and the total
  • I can draw a histogram and find percentage frequencies
