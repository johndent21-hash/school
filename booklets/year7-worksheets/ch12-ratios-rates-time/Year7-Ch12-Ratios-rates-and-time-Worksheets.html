How to use this booklet

1There is one worksheet for each lesson. It has three columns that get harder from left to right: Easy, then Medium, then Challenging.
2Read the speech bubble at the top of each column: it tells you what to do. A new bubble means a new kind of question.
WE DOTop of each column. Your teacher works the examples on the board. Copy every step into the space.
YOU DOThe rest of the column. Your turn. Column 1: answer on the line. Columns 2 and 3: show your working on the lines.

In this chapter you will

  • write, simplify and use ratios
  • solve ratio problems
  • write rates and find the best buy
  • solve rate problems and read travel graphs
  • round, convert and calculate with time
  • use 12-hour and 24-hour time, time differences and timetables

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is a jellyfish of spirals near the neck of the left bulb. Maths can make beautiful things.
