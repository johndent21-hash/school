How to use this booklet

1There is one worksheet for each lesson. It has three columns that get harder from left to right: Easy, then Medium, then Challenging.
2Read the speech bubble at the top of each column: it tells you what to do. A new bubble means a new kind of question.
WE DOTop of each column. Your teacher works the examples on the board. Copy every step into the space.
YOU DOThe rest of the column. Your turn. Column 1: answer on the line. Columns 2 and 3: show your working on the lines.

In this chapter you will

  • check whether a value is a solution
  • solve equations by guess, check and improve
  • solve one-step and two-step equations using inverse operations
  • write and solve equations to solve problems

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is the valley between the main body and the left bulb, where the seahorses start. Maths can make beautiful things.
