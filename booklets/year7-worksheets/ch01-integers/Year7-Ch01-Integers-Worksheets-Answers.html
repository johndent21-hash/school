  • E3 (WE DO) −2 + (−3)

  • 11 −3 + (−6)

Step 3: Zero pairs

  • E4 (WE DO) −5 + 8
    8 − 5 = 3  ·  + has more: 3
  • E5 (WE DO) 6 + (−9)
    9 − 6 = 3  ·  − has more: −3

  • 13 −9 + 4

  • 15 −15 + 20

  • 16 25 + (−40)

  • E6 (WE DO) 1 + (−4) + 5

Step 5: Adding in real life

  • 24 Sam has $15 in the bank. Sam spends $40 using credit. What is the balance now?

  • 25 A diver is at −18 m. She swims up 7 m, then down 4 m. Where is she now?

Step 6: Is it right?

  • E8 (WE DO) Tom says −6 + 2 = −8. Is he right?

  • 26 Mia says −8 + 5 = −13. Is she right?

  • 28 Ali says 6 + (−9) = 3. Is he right?
