How to use this booklet

1Each lesson is one hour. Start with the learning intentions and success criteria, write the key terms and use the notes box.
2Every lesson has three levels: Easy, then Medium, then Challenging. Work down the page: the questions get harder as you go.
WE DOGrey boxes. Your teacher works each example (parts a, b, c) on the board. Copy every step into the space.
YOU DOBlue boxes. Now it is your turn. Work on your own. Quick drill answers go on the line; show working in the boxes.
DRILLSkill drill pages. After the Easy and the Medium examples there is a full page of short practice questions. Work down the page, write answers on the lines, then mark it and record your score and time.
3Finished early? Try the extension. At the end, show off your skill on the tear-off exit ticket and hand it in.
4Missed a lesson? Each lesson ends with a summary with a worked example for every level. Start there.

In this chapter you will

  • measure and draw angles with a protractor
  • classify angles by size
  • find complementary, supplementary and vertically opposite angles
  • construct parallel and perpendicular lines
  • find corresponding, alternate and co-interior angles on parallel lines
  • prove that two lines are parallel

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is Seahorse Valley, the curling tails between the main body and the round bulb on its left. Maths can make beautiful things.

The hands of a clock show 4 o’clock. What is the angle between them? What is the reflex angle?
  1. Each hour mark is 360 ÷ 12 = 30°
  2. 4 hours apart: 4 × 30 = 120°
  3. Reflex: 360 − 120 = 240°

Lesson summaryMissed the lesson? Start here.
Tests for parallel lines
  1. Corresponding angles are equal, or
  2. alternate angles are equal, or
  3. co-interior angles add to 180°.
  4. Write: “AB ∥ CD (alternate angles are equal)”.
Level 1
135° and 45° co-interior?
  1. 135 + 45 = 180

Answer: AB ∥ CD

Level 2
110° and 72° co-interior?
  1. 110 + 72 = 182 ≠ 180

Answer: not parallel

Level 3
74° and 74° corresponding
  1. Equal

Answer: AB ∥ CD

Extension
Co-interior 85° and x°
  1. x = 180 − 85

Answer: x = 95
