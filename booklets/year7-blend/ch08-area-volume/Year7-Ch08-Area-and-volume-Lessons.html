About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is the thin branches (dendrites) that grow from the very top of the set. Maths can make beautiful things.
