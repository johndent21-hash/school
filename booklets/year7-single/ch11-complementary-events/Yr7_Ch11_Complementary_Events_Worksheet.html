Complementary events

Worksheet · work on your own

MA4-PRO-C-01Solves problems involving the probabilities of simple chance experiments

Key words

outcome
one thing that can happen. A die has 6: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
event
what we want to happen, e.g. rolling an even number.
complement
all the other outcomes. It is the event not happening.
not
P(not A) means “the chance that A does not happen”.

How to use this sheet: read each grey Example, then do the blue Round under it. Check your answers at the end of page 2.

Notes: An event and its complement never share an outcome. Together they make all the outcomes, so one of them must happen.

Formulas

P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
P(A) + P(not A) = 1

Tip: 1 whole = 44 = 66 = 1010 = 1.0

A die is rolled. P(rolling a 6) = 16. Find P(not rolling a 6).
1Use the rule:  P(not 6) = 1 − P(6).  Write 1 with the same bottom number:  1 = 66
