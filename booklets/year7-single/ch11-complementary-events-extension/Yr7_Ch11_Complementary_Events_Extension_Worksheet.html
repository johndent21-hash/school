Complementary events

Extension worksheet · work on your own

MA4-PRO-C-01Solves problems involving the probabilities of simple chance experiments

Key words

complement
all the outcomes that are not in the event.
more than 4
5, 6, … (4 is not included). Its complement is “4 or less”.
at least 4
4 or more (4 is included). Its complement is “less than 4”.
prime
has exactly two factors: 2, 3, 5, 7, … (1 is not prime).

How to use this sheet: read each grey Example, then do the blue Round under it. The first question in a round is started for you.

Notes: If the only outcomes are A, B and C, then P(A) + P(B) + P(C) = 1. Probabilities are never below 0 or above 1.

Formulas

P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
P(A) + P(not A) = 1

Tip: 1 = 88 = 1.00 = 100%
